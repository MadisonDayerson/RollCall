How to install RollCall
=======================

RollCall needs a Mac running macOS 14 (Sonoma) or newer. It works on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

1. Drag RollCall into the Applications folder (in this window).
2. Open Applications and double-click RollCall.

The first time, your Mac says RollCall can't be opened, because it isn't from the App Store
and hasn't been checked by Apple. That's expected. To open it anyway:

3. Click Done (or OK) on that message.
4. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
5. Scroll down to the message about RollCall and click Open Anyway.
6. Enter your Mac password, then click Open Anyway again.

You only need to do this once. RollCall then opens its setup, which walks you through:

  • your home area, so you only see rides nearby
  • your group chats, through the free Beeper app (WhatsApp, Messenger, Messages and more)
  • the clubs, groups, forums and Instagram accounts you follow
  • Apple Calendar or Google Calendar for the rides you're going to

RollCall lives in the menu bar at the top of the screen (the gothic RC).
Everything stays on your Mac: RollCall never posts, sends messages or RSVPs for you.

Plan summaries for chats use Apple Intelligence where it's available (macOS 26 on Apple silicon);
other Macs get RollCall's own simpler summaries.
