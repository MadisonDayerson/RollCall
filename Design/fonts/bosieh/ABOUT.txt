Bosieh by imoodev, from https://www.1001fonts.com/bosieh-font.html
Licence: 1001Fonts Free For Personal Use (demo font, no commercial use).

It's only used to draw the RollCall icon (Design/RollCall.png and Design/MenuIcon.png) and the "Roll Call"
wordmark at the foot of the window's sidebar (Design/Wordmark.png) and the "RC" on empty pages
(Design/Monogram.png), which are in the repository. The font file itself isn't in the repository: to redraw the icon, download
bosieh.regular.otf from the page above, save it here as Bosieh-Regular.otf, and run
  swift Design/make_icon.swift Design/RollCall.png
  swift Design/make_wordmark.swift Design/Wordmark.png
  swift Design/make_wordmark.swift Design/Monogram.png RC
