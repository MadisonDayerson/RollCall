## Setup

The setup guide opens the first time. Open it again with **Set up RollCall…** in the menu.

1. **Your area:** your suburb or town, and how far you'll travel.
2. **Chats:** connect group chats through the free Beeper app (see [Group chats](#group-chats)).
3. **Groups & accounts:** pick from suggestions for your area, or paste any link or `@instagram` name.
4. **Calendar:** allow access, then choose iCloud, Google, Exchange or this Mac.
   For Google, first add your Google account in **System Settings → Internet Accounts**.
5. **Riding together:** your crew's location app, voice chat, Discord and tags.

Each permission (your calendar, and notifications) is asked for once, at the step that needs it, and kept over updates.

## Rides

Going adds the ride to your **RollCall** calendar, with a reminder the evening before. While the window is open,
⌘1–⌘4 switch pages.

**Rides**
- A ride posted in several places shows once, with every place under its title as its **Source**.
- **All** shows Undecided (including Maybe), Going, Skipped and Past in sections you can fold away.
- Choose **Going**, **Maybe** or **Skip** at the top of a ride's page. Choosing the chosen one again leaves it undecided.
- A ? on the date means it was read from a post, so double-check it.
- **About** has when, where and a plain summary; **See more** shows the whole description.
- **⋯** on a ride in the list (or a right-click) has **Open**, **Open route in**, **Edit…** and **Remove**.
  Your edits stay when the source is checked again. Remove takes the ride away for good, even if its source still lists it.
- Rides you went on move to **Past**, and are deleted after a week (up to two: Settings → Privacy).

**To review** holds plans from your chats.
- **Possible plans** are a ride or bike event on a day still to come, with a time and a place.
- **Partial plans** are missing one of those. Ride posts that didn't say when are here too.
- RollCall keeps reading a conversation until the ride is over (or for four days while there's no day yet, and never
  past two weeks of quiet). After a quiet spell, only a message giving the plan's day, a time or a meeting place picks it up again.
- A partial plan moves up, with a notification, once it's complete.
- Plans you mark **Not a ride** wait under **Reviewed** for a week, in case you change your mind.

**Sources** is everything RollCall reads, grouped by app.
- Chats are checked every 15 minutes, calendars every 3 hours, and websites and forums every 6 hours. Change any of
  them with **Check for new rides** (right-click a heading to change a whole group).
- Instagram and Facebook you check yourself.
- Change a source's name or link on its page. Remove it with the Delete key, **Remove…** or a right-click.

**Settings** has your home area, chats, Facebook link, calendar, riding together, road alerts, privacy and updates.

## Notifications

RollCall notifies you once about each new ride from your sources and, on the Mac, each complete plan in a chat.

- **Going** puts it straight in your calendar, and a second notification says so. **Review** opens it in RollCall.
- On the Mac, the buttons show when you point at the notification. On iPhone and iPad, touch and hold it.
- More than three new rides at once make one notification.
- You aren't told about rides too far away, ones you added yourself, another posting of a ride you have, a regular
  ride's next date, or what was already in a source you've just added.
- Once you've decided on a ride, on any device, its notification goes.

## Adding rides and sources

**+ → Add a Source…**: paste a link and RollCall works out what it is, then keeps checking it.

| Paste | Result |
|---|---|
| Club or event website, forum section, Meetup group, calendar link (`.ics`, `webcal://`) or news feed | Checked automatically |
| Your Facebook events calendar link | See [Facebook and Instagram](#facebook-and-instagram) |
| Instagram account (`@name`) | Added to the accounts you check |

**Check chats** is here too (see [Group chats](#group-chats)).

**+ → Add a Ride…** adds one ride:
- **Paste an event poster:** copy the picture (right-click → Copy Image, or ⌃⇧⌘4 for a screenshot). RollCall reads it
  on your Mac and fills in the name, date, time and meeting place for you to check. With nothing copied, you choose a file.
- **Paste a post or message:** its text, or an Instagram post's link.
- **Type a ride in**, **Import a calendar file** or **Add a Facebook event**.

## Facebook and Instagram

**Your Facebook events.** Facebook can share a private calendar of the events you're Going or Interested in. Connect
it in setup's **Groups & accounts** step, **Settings → Facebook** or **Sources → Facebook events**:

1. Click **Open Facebook Events**, find **Add to calendar**, right-click it and choose **Copy Link**.
2. Paste the link into RollCall and click **Connect**.

Going on Facebook becomes Going in RollCall, and Interested becomes Maybe. RollCall follows if you change your reply,
unless you've chosen something else in RollCall, which always wins. Keep the link to yourself: anyone who has it can see
your events.

**One Facebook event at a time:**

1. Click **+** → **Add a Ride…** → **Add a Facebook event**, paste the event link, then click **Open event page**.
2. On Facebook, click **⋯** → **Add to calendar** → **Download**.
3. The ride appears in RollCall within a minute. (Allow access to Downloads if asked.)

**Instagram** doesn't let apps read posts. Open the account from **Sources**, copy a ride post's caption (⌘C), then click
**+** → **Add a Ride…** → **Paste a post or message**.

## Group chats

WhatsApp, Messenger and Messages don't let other apps read chats. [Beeper](https://www.beeper.com/download) brings them
into one app and lets RollCall read only the chats you choose, read-only. **Set up Beeper**, in setup's Chats step,
walks you through this and checks each step:

1. Install Beeper and connect your chat accounts in **Beeper → Settings → Accounts**.
2. In Beeper, open **Settings → Developers** and switch on **Beeper Desktop API**.
3. In RollCall, click **+** → **Add a Source…** → **Check chats**, click **Allow** in Beeper, then tick your ride chats.

Beeper needs to be open for chats to be checked.

When a chat mentions a day or time for a ride, RollCall sums up the plan (what, when, where, who's in) and asks you to
review it. Later replies join the same plan, and a ride you added from it takes a new time or place if the chat changes it
(your own edits stay). A message sent to several of your chats shows once. **Add ride…** fills in the details for you to
check, using Apple Intelligence on your Mac where available.

## Finding more details

With Apple Intelligence on, **Refresh** (↻) checks every source, then looks for more about the rides you're going to or
might go to, and plans in **To review** that have a day. It reads their event pages, the last two weeks of your chats and
your other sources. The automatic checks don't do this.

What it finds (time, meeting place, destination, stops, cost or link, and where each came from) shows under **More
details** on the ride's page. Click **Add** for the ones you want. For a plan, the finds fill in the **Add ride…** form,
apart from any you **Leave out**. While it looks, a spinner shows next to the ride and the **Roll Call** at the foot of
the sidebar glows.

## Routes and weather

Each ride's page has a map of the road route with its distance and riding time. Click it to open the route in the maps
app chosen in Settings.

- **The route** is the organiser's own Google Maps or Apple Maps route when they shared one. Otherwise RollCall works it
  out from the ride's title and description, and leaves out places it can't find. A ride at one place, like a track
  day, shows the trip there from your suburb.
- **Open route in** gives directions in Apple Maps, Google Maps, Waze or Kurviger. Apple Maps and Waze only take the
  meeting place. The organiser's own route is at the top of the list when there is one.
- **Weather** shows the temperature, chance of rain and wind every 40 to 50 km, for when you'd be there. Likely rain and
  strong gusts are in orange. Rides more than a week away say when their forecast will be ready.

## Road alerts

In Victoria, a ride's page can show crashes, closures, hazards and roadworks on its roads, for when you'd be riding
them. They're listed under **Road alerts on N roads** and marked on the map: point at an icon (tap it on iPhone and
iPad) for a summary. Alerts happening now show for rides within a day. Booked roadworks take about a minute to download the
first time, and are then refreshed every six hours.

They come from the Department of Transport and Planning's open data and need a free key of your own:

1. Go to the [Transport Victoria Open Data Portal](https://opendata.transport.vic.gov.au/), click **Login**, then
   **Sign up**. Enter your details, then the code they email you.
2. The portal asks you to set up an authenticator app on your phone (any one works, such as Google Authenticator
   or Microsoft Authenticator): scan the QR code and enter the code it shows.
3. Log in, click **My Account → Profile**, then **API tokens**, and copy your key.
4. In RollCall, paste it into **Settings → Road alerts** and click **Save**. Do this on each device.

**Road alerts on routes**, in the same place, shows **Current and planned**, **Current only** (no booked roadworks) or
**None**. The last two delete any booked roadworks already downloaded.

## Riding together

The calendar event for a ride you're going to includes the map link, your crew's Discord, voice chat group and a
location-sharing reminder.

- **Voice chat** is your helmet intercom (Cardo, Sena, Interphone, UClear, Midland, FreedConn, LEXIN) or a call
  (Discord voice, WhatsApp). Intercoms pair with your phone, so set up the group in its phone app, then add the group or
  channel name in Settings → Riding together.
- **Tags:** every ride gets #RC and its date, like `#RC03112026`, the same for everyone using RollCall. Tags for the club,
  brand, place or occasion follow it, so everyone tags their photos and videos the same way.

Life360, Cardo and the social apps don't let other apps connect, so RollCall puts the details in the calendar event
rather than showing friends' locations or posting for you.

## iPhone and iPad

The iPhone and iPad version is in progress. To put it on your own device, see
[how to install it](DEVELOPING.md#iphone-and-ipad).

It reads the same clubs, calendars, forums and event sites by itself, with the same route map, weather, road alerts and
notifications. Pulling the list down refreshes it and, with Apple Intelligence on, looks for more details (not in chats).

- **Setup** goes through everything one step at a time; every step after your area can be skipped. Settings → **Run
  setup…** opens it again.
- **Group chats** are read on the Mac only, because Beeper only lets other apps read chats on a computer. Rides from
  chats arrive without their messages.
- **Notifications** turn on in Settings. While RollCall is closed, iOS decides when it can check for rides, so a new
  one can take a few hours. Swiping RollCall away in the app switcher stops the checks until it's opened again.

**Sharing with the Mac:**

1. On the Mac, turn on Settings → iPhone and iPad → **Share with RollCall on iPhone and iPad** (iCloud Drive needs to be on).
   That makes a RollCall folder in iCloud Drive.
2. On the iPhone or iPad, tap Settings → **Share with RollCall on Mac…** and choose that folder.

A device that joins takes on what the Mac has. Rides, choices, ride changes and sources then go both ways, and the later
change wins. A ride you're going to is added to the RollCall calendar once, even when both devices add it.
